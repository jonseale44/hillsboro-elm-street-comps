What those rates imply for 16,250 SF

Smaller buildings usually sell for more per square foot. 100 W Elm is a restaurant of 3,834 SF. 100 E Elm is 16,250 SF of restaurant, bar, and apartments. Applying a restaurant rate to every foot of the larger building prices apartment space as dining room.

100 E Elm has a working kitchen, a bar, and occupied apartments. That supports a higher price per foot than a dry-cleaner and office building. It does not, by itself, put restaurant pricing on every square foot of a 16,000 SF mixed-use building.
